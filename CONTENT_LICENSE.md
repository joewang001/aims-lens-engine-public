
Include a link to this repository and indicate material changes when applicable.

## Source Handling

Public lens summaries must be non-verbatim, evidence-backed, and source-aware.
Do not copy raw articles, job postings, paywalled content, private employer
documents, user-submitted job bodies, candidate answers, resumes, or tenant
uploads into public lens files.
