# Google Lens Limits

## Public Lens Notice

This lens is inferred from public sources and private-material distillation. It does not represent an official Google hiring standard.

## Current Limits

- v0.1 synthesis draft only.
- Static validation cases have passed, but no live model scoring run has been completed.
- Google Careers pages exposed limited detailed process text in accessible HTML; process patterns rely partly on lower-confidence private material distillation.
- Project-owner-authorized private material is included only as aggregate distilled evidence; raw text, copied questions, and private URLs are excluded from public lens files.
- Role-specific differences may be material and must be captured before production use.
- Region-specific hiring, hiring committee, recruiter process, and team matching can vary.

## Production Blockers

- Complete role-specific overlays for SWE, SRE, AI/ML, Data Analyst, UI/Frontend, PM, and Google Cloud customer-facing technical roles.
- Run same-answer cross-lens validation against Amazon and later company lenses.
- Run live transcript tests and review outputs for accuracy, bias, and unsupported claims.
- Complete human review of `profile.json`, `rubric.md`, and `validation_report.md`.
