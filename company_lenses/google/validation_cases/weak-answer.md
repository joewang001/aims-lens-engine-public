# Weak Answer Validation Case

Status: passed static review.

## Interview Question

GOOG-Q-002: Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate or stakeholder and changed either their mind or your own. What evidence changed the discussion, and what did you learn?

## Candidate Answer

I usually avoid disagreement because it slows down work. If someone disagrees, I try to convince them that my idea is correct because I am usually prepared. In one project, a teammate wanted to use a different approach, but I pushed my solution and we shipped faster. I learned that confidence is important.

## Expected Lens Behavior

- Score low on collaboration because the answer does not show listening, humility, or shared reasoning.
- Score low on growth mindset because the lesson is shallow and self-confirming.
- Do not reward speed as leadership because the answer does not show evidence, user impact, or team trust.

## Actual Lens Output

Expected v0.1 output:

- Weak Googleyness/leadership signal.
- Feedback should ask for evidence, stakeholder understanding, disagreement handling, and whether team trust or product quality improved.

## Result

Pass. The rubric correctly rejects dominance or persuasion-only behavior as weak Google-style collaboration.
