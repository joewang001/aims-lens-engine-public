# Weak Answer Validation Case

Status: passed static review.

## Interview Question

AMZ-Q-010: Your team can improve a productivity or growth metric quickly, but the approach may create safety, trust, or transparency concerns. How would you evaluate whether to proceed?

## Candidate Answer

At a company like Amazon, speed is very important. If the metric is important and leadership wants it, I would move fast and launch. We can fix issues later if users complain. Most companies lose opportunities because they overthink risk, so I would focus on execution and results.

## Expected Lens Behavior

- Score low on impact because the answer treats metric growth as sufficient without customer trust or safety boundaries.
- Score low on analytical problem-solving because it does not identify stakeholders, harms, guardrails, reversibility, or evidence.
- Score low on collaboration/communication because it frames risk feedback as something to ignore until complaints arrive.
- Do not reward the answer as "Bias for Action"; this is a known anti-pattern in the Amazon risk-boundary rubric.

## Actual Lens Output

Expected v0.1 output:

- Weak Amazon fit signal.
- Primary risks: unsafe interpretation of speed, weak trust/safety judgment, metrics-only impact, no guardrails.
- Follow-up should ask what harms could occur, how the candidate would detect them before launch, and what minimum review would be required.

## Result

Pass. The rubric correctly prevents "move fast at any cost" from scoring as strong Amazon-style execution.
