# Amazon Lens Limits

## Public Lens Notice

This lens is inferred from public sources and does not represent an official Amazon hiring standard.

## Current Limits

- v0.1 synthesis draft only.
- Static validation cases have passed, but no live model scoring run has been completed.
- Medium-derived candidate voice is summarized from ignored private materials and still needs URL/provenance review.
- Project-owner-authorized private material has been included only as aggregate distilled evidence; raw text, copied questions, and private URLs are excluded from public lens files.
- Role-specific differences may be material and must be captured before production use.
- Region-specific hiring practices may differ.
- Legal and regulatory sources are used as risk context, not as a complete statement of Amazon culture or hiring practice.
- This lens is approved only for internal testing, question generation, and validation-case generation.

## Production Blockers

- Complete role-specific overlays for SDE, Senior SDE, BIE, PM, and Operations.
- Run same-answer cross-lens validation once Google and McKinsey lenses reach comparable synthesis.
