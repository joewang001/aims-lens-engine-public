# TD Bank Rubric

Status: v0.1 synthesis draft. Static validation completed; production use not approved.

## Scoring Posture

TD weighting should emphasize client/community impact, risk ownership, customer empathy, simplicity, inclusion, and role alignment. A strong answer protects customer trust and risk culture while showing practical service or operational improvement.

| Dimension | Weight | TD interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Structured Thinking | 0.15 | Role-aligned story, clear situation/action/result, risk and customer context. |
| Analytical Problem-Solving | 0.17 | Banking judgment, risk assessment, suitability, process improvement, and data-informed decisions. |
| Ownership & Execution | 0.20 | Owns issues, raises concerns, follows through, and improves customer/operational outcomes. |
| Impact & Results | 0.20 | Better outcomes for clients, colleagues, communities, and the bank. |
| Collaboration & Communication | 0.16 | Remarkably human and refreshingly simple communication, empathy, inclusion, and stakeholder alignment. |
| Growth Mindset | 0.12 | Career growth, learning, mentoring, and adapting under regulated-service constraints. |

## Role Overlays

| Role family | Strong evidence to seek | Do not over-credit |
|---|---|---|
| Retail / Personal Banking | Customer empathy, suitability, compliance, service-to-sales judgment, difficult interactions. | Sales numbers without customer fit or risk awareness. |
| Contact Center | De-escalation, clarity, process knowledge, empathy, escalation. | Script-following without judgment or care. |
| Risk / Operations | Own it, Raise it, Know it, process controls, documentation, escalation. | Speed or productivity that hides operational risk. |
| Technology / Corporate | Simplification, execution, stakeholder communication, risk-aware delivery. | Technical implementation without bank/customer risk context. |
| All roles | Better Bank contribution, role alignment, inclusion, community/client impact. | Generic banking interest or generic customer service claims. |

## Private Material Distillation Rule

Private materials may contribute only aggregate patterns, confidence levels, original questions, evidence IDs, and limitations. Public files must not include article bodies, copied question lists, private URLs, or unnecessary author details.

## Production Rule

Internal v0.1 testing only. Production use requires live transcript validation, same-answer cross-lens testing, provenance review, and human sign-off.
