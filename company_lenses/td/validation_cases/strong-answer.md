## Interview Question

TD-Q-003: You notice a process shortcut that helps the team move faster but may create operational or customer risk. How would you decide what to do?

## Candidate Answer

I would first understand what risk the shortcut creates: whether it affects customer information, suitability, compliance, or downstream operations. If the risk could harm customers or the bank, I would not wait for it to become an incident. I would document examples, assess likely impact, and raise it to my manager or risk partner with a practical alternative.

I would also ask why the shortcut exists. If the official process is too slow, I would propose a simpler approved workflow or automation so the team can keep speed without bypassing controls. The goal is to own the issue, raise it early, and know the policy well enough to improve the process.

- Strong TD fit signal.
- High ownership, risk judgment, customer trust, and simplification.
