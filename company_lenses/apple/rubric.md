# Apple Rubric

Status: v0.1 synthesis draft. Static validation completed; production use not approved.

## Scoring Posture

Apple weighting should emphasize product craft, role-specific depth, user experience, privacy, accessibility, and cross-functional quality. Strong answers connect technical decisions to user-facing excellence and values.

| Dimension | Weight | Apple interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Structured Thinking | 0.17 | Clear product/technical framing, constraints, alternatives, and validation plan. |
| Analytical Problem-Solving | 0.20 | Deep role-specific reasoning with performance, platform, privacy, accessibility, and implementation trade-offs. |
| Ownership & Execution | 0.16 | Drives quality improvements and decisions without losing practical execution. |
| Impact & Results | 0.19 | User experience, trust, accessibility, product reliability, and measurable quality. |
| Collaboration & Communication | 0.14 | Cross-functional clarity with design, hardware, software, product, operations, and customer teams. |
| Growth Mindset | 0.14 | Iteration, feedback response, craft improvement, and learning under a high quality bar. |

## Role Overlays

| Role family | Strong evidence to seek | Do not over-credit |
|---|---|---|
| iOS / Frontend | Platform depth, performance, accessibility, code quality, UX polish. | Code mechanics without user-facing quality. |
| Product Design | User problem framing, critique, iteration, accessibility, platform consistency. | Beautiful visuals without evidence or trade-offs. |
| AI/ML | Evaluation quality, privacy, on-device constraints, user usefulness, fallback behavior. | Better offline model metric without product/privacy reasoning. |
| All roles | Craft, values, collaboration, practical quality mechanisms. | Generic Apple admiration or perfection language without evidence. |

## Private Material Distillation Rule

Private materials can strengthen the lens only as aggregate signals, confidence levels, original generated questions, and evidence IDs. Public files must not include copied article bodies, copied question lists, private URLs, or unnecessary author-specific details.

## Production Rule

This rubric is for internal v0.1 testing only. Production use requires live transcript validation, role-specific overlays, provenance review, and human sign-off.
