# Strong Answer Validation Case

Status: passed static review.

## Interview Question

MSFT-Q-001: Tell me about a time feedback showed that your first approach was not good enough. How did you respond, what did you change, and what improved afterward?

## Candidate Answer

In a platform migration project, my first design reduced deployment time but made troubleshooting harder for support engineers. A teammate told me the design optimized for my engineering workflow but not for the people operating it. I initially disagreed, but I reviewed support tickets and shadowed two incidents.

I changed the design to add clearer logs, a rollback command, and a runbook. Deployment time was still 35% faster, and incident resolution time dropped by about 20% in the next month. I also added support-readiness review to later migrations.

- Positive signal for learning from feedback and empowering another team.
