# Microsoft Rubric

Status: v0.1 synthesis draft. Static validation completed; production use not approved.

## Scoring Posture

Microsoft weighting should emphasize analytical problem solving, growth mindset, customer/organization empowerment, collaboration, and responsible AI/trust. A strong answer shows how the candidate learns, clarifies, builds, tests, and improves outcomes.

| Dimension | Weight | Microsoft interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Structured Thinking | 0.17 | Clarifies problem, constraints, evidence, plan, and validation. |
| Analytical Problem-Solving | 0.22 | Analyzes, investigates, designs, builds, tests, and reasons through data/results. |
| Ownership & Execution | 0.16 | Converts learning and analysis into practical improvements. |
| Impact & Results | 0.18 | Empowers customers, teams, or organizations with measurable and trusted outcomes. |
| Collaboration & Communication | 0.14 | Works across functions and explains trade-offs clearly. |
| Growth Mindset | 0.13 | Learns from feedback, adapts, and improves without defensiveness. |

## Role Overlays

| Role family | Strong evidence to seek | Do not over-credit |
|---|---|---|
| SDE / SWE | Clarification, debugging, system design, testing, maintainability, communication. | Code answer without reasoning or tests. |
| Cloud / Solution Architecture | Customer discovery, security, reliability, adoption, stakeholder communication. | Generic cloud architecture without customer context. |
| Data | Data quality, SQL/pipeline reasoning, metric trust, business decision impact. | Query mechanics without data trust and business meaning. |
| AI/ML | Responsible AI principles, evaluation, monitoring, privacy/security, accountability. | Productivity claim without trust boundaries. |
| All roles | Growth mindset, feedback, collaboration, customer empowerment. | Fixed-mindset defensiveness or generic Microsoft admiration. |

## Private Material Distillation Rule

Private materials may contribute only aggregate patterns, confidence levels, original questions, evidence IDs, and limitations. Public files must not include article bodies, copied question lists, private URLs, or unnecessary author details.

## Production Rule

Internal v0.1 testing only. Production use requires live transcript validation, same-answer cross-lens testing, provenance review, and human sign-off.
