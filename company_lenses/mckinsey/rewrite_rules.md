# McKinsey Rewrite Rules

Status: draft placeholder.

## Purpose

Rewrite candidate answers through the approved McKinsey lens after evidence validation.

## Draft Rules To Validate

- Start with a clear thesis.
- Organize evidence into two or three prioritized reasons.
- Make assumptions and business logic explicit.
- Connect actions to client, business, or organizational impact.
- Avoid rambling chronology without a decision structure.

## Boundary

These rules are not approved until supporting evidence is logged and validated.
