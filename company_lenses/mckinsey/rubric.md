# McKinsey Rubric

Status: v0.1 synthesis draft. Static validation completed; production use not approved.

## Scoring Posture

McKinsey weighting should emphasize structured problem solving, analytical rigor, client-ready communication, personal impact, and ethical dissent. The answer should be hypothesis-driven and recommendation-oriented.

| Dimension | Weight | McKinsey interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Structured Thinking | 0.28 | Issue trees, hypotheses, synthesis, top-down communication, and logical sequencing. |
| Analytical Problem-Solving | 0.25 | Quantitative reasoning, prioritization, data interpretation, and case judgment. |
| Ownership & Execution | 0.12 | Personal impact, drive, and practical next steps. |
| Impact & Results | 0.15 | Client or organizational value, measurable outcomes, and implementation realism. |
| Collaboration & Communication | 0.15 | Client-ready clarity, teamwork, dissent, and stakeholder management. |
| Growth Mindset | 0.05 | Learning matters, but is less weighted than problem solving and synthesis. |

## Role Overlays

| Role family | Strong evidence to seek | Do not over-credit |
|---|---|---|
| Consulting / Strategy | MECE structure, hypothesis, math, synthesis, recommendation. | Framework name-dropping without tailored logic. |
| PEI / Behavioral | One deep story with stakes, action, conflict, impact, reflection. | Resume summary or multiple shallow examples. |
| Digital / Analytics | Business value plus technical feasibility, adoption, data, and risk. | Tech solution without client economics or change path. |
| All roles | Integrity, dissent, client impact, concise communication. | Telling clients what they want to hear despite evidence. |

## Private Material Distillation Rule

Private materials may contribute only aggregate patterns, confidence levels, original questions, evidence IDs, and limitations. Public files must not include article bodies, copied question lists, private URLs, or unnecessary author details.

## Case Book Distillation Addendum

Project-owner distilled case-interview book notes may strengthen the case rubric only as aggregate heuristics:

| Signal | Scoring use | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Objective-first case opening | Credit candidates who clarify goal, scope, metric, and constraints before structuring. | MCK-BOOK-001 |
| Tailored issue tree and prioritization | Credit custom structure and reasoned first-branch choice; do not over-credit named frameworks. | MCK-BOOK-001 |
| Exhibit/math to insight | Credit candidates who state the business implication, sanity check, and next analysis after numbers. | MCK-BOOK-002 |
| Interim synthesis | Credit concise synthesis during the case before final recommendation. | MCK-BOOK-001, MCK-BOOK-002 |
| Deliberate practice loop | Use for coaching and calibration notes; do not use as final screening evidence. | MCK-BOOK-003 |

## Production Rule

Internal v0.1 testing only. Production use requires live transcript validation, same-answer cross-lens testing, provenance review, and human sign-off.
