## Interview Question

MCK-Q-001: A retailer's online sales are growing but profit is declining. How would you structure the problem, what analyses would you prioritize, and what recommendation would you give after initial findings?

## Candidate Answer

I would start with profit equals revenue minus cost, then split online revenue into traffic, conversion, average order value, and returns. On cost, I would separate product margin, fulfillment, customer acquisition, promotions, and customer service. My first hypothesis is that growth is being bought through expensive acquisition or promotions, or that fulfillment/returns are eroding contribution margin.

I would prioritize segmenting profit by customer cohort and order type, because overall online growth can hide unprofitable segments. If initial data shows new customers acquired through paid channels have 20% lower margin and double the return rate, I would recommend tightening acquisition spend, testing minimum-order or return-policy changes, and improving product-page fit signals before scaling further.
