# JPMorgan Chase Rubric

Status: v0.1 synthesis draft. Static validation completed; production use not approved.

## Scoring Posture

This lens emphasizes client/customer impact, role alignment, conduct and risk boundaries, collaboration, and practical execution in a regulated financial-services context.

## Dimension Rules

- Structured Thinking: clear story/case framing, role alignment, constraints, and evidence.
- Analytical Problem-Solving: banking judgment, risk assessment, data/process reasoning, and trade-offs.
- Ownership & Execution: owns issues, escalates appropriately, follows through, and improves process or client outcomes.
- Impact & Results: better outcomes for clients/customers, teams, communities, and the institution.
- Collaboration & Communication: clear, empathetic, professional communication across stakeholders.
- Growth Mindset: learning, coaching, adaptation, and career development.

## Private Material Distillation Rule

Private materials may contribute only aggregate patterns, confidence levels, original questions, evidence IDs, and limitations. Public files must not include article bodies, copied question lists, private URLs, or unnecessary author details.

## Production Rule

Internal v0.1 testing only. Production use requires live transcript validation, same-answer cross-lens testing, provenance review, and human sign-off.
