# Shopify Rubric

## Strong Signals

- Connects work to merchant, entrepreneur, or commerce outcomes.
- Shows builder ownership: problem, shipped action, result, and learning.
- Explains tradeoffs with clear reasoning and practical execution detail.
- Demonstrates curiosity, adaptability, and willingness to grow through difficult problems.
- Communicates clearly without hiding behind generic process language.

## Medium Signals

- Shows useful execution but weak connection to merchant impact.
- Describes learning but without enough before/after evidence.
- Mentions customer or business impact in general terms but lacks mechanism.

## Weak Signals

- Generic interest in ecommerce or Shopify without role-specific fit.
- Process-heavy story with no concrete shipping, outcome, or learning.
- Comfort-seeking answer that avoids ambiguity, tradeoffs, or hard feedback.

## AIMS Emphasis

- `ownership_execution`: high
- `impact_results`: high
- `analytical_problem_solving`: medium-high
- `structured_thinking`: medium
- `collaboration_communication`: medium
- `growth_mindset`: medium
