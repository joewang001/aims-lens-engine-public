# Shopify Rewrite Rules

## Improve Toward Shopify Fit

- Replace generic ecommerce interest with a specific merchant or entrepreneur problem.
- Add builder evidence: what was shipped, what changed, and what was learned.
- Make tradeoffs explicit: speed, quality, scope, risk, and merchant impact.
- Avoid sounding comfort-seeking; show adaptation under ambiguity.
- Keep claims public-safe and do not imply official Shopify hiring standards.

## Avoid

- Copying public or private interview questions verbatim.
- Referring to private source paths.
- Claiming this lens reflects internal Shopify hiring policy.
